How to read the examples.

Names, business facts and arithmetic in worked examples are fictional practice inputs. They show deliverables, not customer results or expected earnings.

No fixed number of posts, messages or days guarantees a sale. Track actual interest, purchases, costs and available payouts separately.

No universal Instagram carousel boost is claimed. Use the format to teach a clear step and compare your own buyer actions.

Build one useful output. Check it. Put it in front of the right person.
