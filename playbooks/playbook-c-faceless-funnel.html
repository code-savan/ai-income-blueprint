START / TOOL ROUTE
Before you begin

Start with the tools you have.

The output stays the same. Choose how you’ll produce it.

Free workflow

Start with a free writing tool and one free editor: Docs, Sheets, Slides, Canva or a video editor. Use your own files or assets you have permission to use.

Finish one practice output before buying anything. Free-tier limits can change. Save your work and test a free export first.

Paid workflow

Paid AI, design or research tools are optional. Pay for a specific problem, such as a longer source file or repeated editing task.

Read the current plan, country support and credit cost in Tools & costs. Budget for retries. A subscription is not a fixed number of finished deliverables or sales.

Check the current plan and your actual bottleneck before spending.

Using your own offer?

Follow the main steps above. Use the closest example in the selector as a reference. You don’t have to enter a name to choose your own offer. Before selling, write the exact output, required facts and one check. Ask the working prompt to clarify anything you do not know.

A complete 30-second demonstration

  1. 0–3 seconds: show the finished result. Say “Here is [specific output]”. Keep the file or item readable.
  2. 3–7 seconds: show the starting problem. Say why this one step matters to the buyer.
  3. 7–18 seconds: show two real actions. Film the click, edit, filled field or product use while you explain it. Cut waiting time.
  4. 18–25 seconds: show the human check. Correct one line, tap the link, check the total or test the fit.
  5. 25–30 seconds: show the final output and one next action. Example: “The preview shows this first task. Open it from my profile.” Use only a link/path that actually exists.
Prompt to turn your actual work into a shot list
I made/tested [actual output] for [buyer task]. These are the exact actions I took [steps]. Turn this into a 30-second demonstration with five shots: result, starting problem, two making/use actions, check and next step. Give timestamps, exact screen/hand action, spoken line, short text and filename. Ask about unknown facts first. No invented buyer, revenue or product performance. Keep the total voiceover short enough to read naturally.
Produce thisOne before/after sentence and a finished screen or product to film.
Pass checkThe viewer can see the result your hook describes.
Your working prompt
My chosen offer is [offer]. I have actually made or tested [output]. Give five specific video angles with a visible before/after and one useful lesson. Use only [verified process]. For each say what must appear on screen to support the claim. No income, reach, customer or product-performance claims I cannot show.

When you get stuck
I do not know what to make

Open Choose my offer. Pick one small service or product, then use the matching example on each step. Make the smallest version you can check.

AI gives a vague answer

Paste the actual facts and required output. Say which sentence or step is unclear. Ask it to show one filled example and exact next action.

I finished it but nobody bought

Review who saw it, what they asked and where they stopped. Ask a relevant person to try the preview. Change one thing, then test again. Do not treat views as sales.
