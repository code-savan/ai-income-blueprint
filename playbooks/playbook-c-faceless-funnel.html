Free workflow

Record your screen and your own voice. Use your own footage or Pexels clips. Edit with available free features in VN or CapCut; create slides in Canva Free.

Check every selected feature and export before a batch. If captions require a paid tier, type and time them manually. Use licensed audio suitable for the account and intended use.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally helps script larger batches. Higgsfield optionally supplies generated scene inserts. Keep screen demonstrations as the evidence.

Calculate credits per usable scene and retries before subscribing. Build a storyboard first. Generated people are not customer testimonials.

Subscriptions do not include a promise of income. Check the live plan and make the same quality checks.

01

Pick one observable transformation

Use a result you can show: a blank calendar becomes seven usable posts, or a vague headline becomes clear copy. Record the finished output first so your hook has a real payoff. Avoid unverified revenue or engagement claims.

Copy and adapt
My offer is [offer]. I can demonstrate [finished output]. Give five before/after video angles for [audience]. Each must be truthful, specific, and visible on screen. Exclude income promises and claims I cannot demonstrate.
Produce thisOne before-and-after sentence and a proof shot.
Pass checkThe footage supports the hook without relying on a fictional result.
02

Write seven useful scripts

Use a hook, problem, short demonstration, and next step. Mix mistakes, walkthroughs, comparisons, and FAQs. Keep the voiceover short enough to match your natural speaking speed. Read each out loud before recording.

Copy and adapt
Create seven 25-40 second scripts for [audience] about [offer]. Source material: [verified process]. Mix two mistakes, two tutorials, one comparison, one FAQ, one demonstration. For each provide voiceover, scene sequence, on-screen text, and one CTA. No invented proof.
Produce thisSeven scripts with a shot list for each.
Pass checkEach teaches or demonstrates one useful action rather than repeating a sales pitch.
03

Capture a reusable footage bank

Record five clean clips: brief, prompt, output, edits, and final deliverable. Hide account details. Record with a readable zoom. For stock, search the specific action, inspect the license, and keep source links. Do not pretend stock shows a client.

Copy and adapt
Turn [script] into a shot list. Label each shot SCREEN, OWN FOOTAGE, STOCK, or GENERATED. Give duration, what it proves, and text overlay. Prioritize actual output. Include a stock search phrase only when needed.
Produce thisA labeled folder of source clips and assets.
Pass checkThe screen remains readable in a phone preview and every visual has a clear role.
04

Build one complete edit

Create a 1080×1920 project. Place the payoff early, then show key steps. Add captions, check spelling and safe margins, balance voice/music, and export. Use still-image motion if you do not need video generation.

Copy and adapt
Review this edit plan: [timestamps and scenes]. Identify slow openings, missing explanation, unreadable text, and mismatches between speech and visuals. Suggest specific cuts. Keep the finished demonstration and a single next step.
Produce thisOne finished vertical video and a reusable project.
Pass checkThe video makes sense muted and spoken claims match the screen.
05

Publish and track buyer actions

Schedule one useful piece daily. Reuse the clean original across platforms. Record reach, profile visits, attributable clicks, conversations, and sales. Compare rates as well as totals. Treat the first seven posts as a learning batch.

Copy and adapt
Analyze these actual post metrics: [rows]. Separate reach problems, click problems, and purchase problems. Suggest one change for the next batch and explain uncertainty from the small sample. Do not claim views imply sales.
Produce thisSeven posts and a filled Daily Content Tracker.
Pass checkYou have an explicit numerator and denominator for each rate. Missing metrics stay blank, not invented.

When you get stuck

Views but no clicks

Check whether the audience, demonstrated output, and offer match. Show the next step clearly and make the profile link understandable.

No footage

Use the sample pack and record the process. Three readable screen shots beat unrelated filler.

Production is slow

Reuse footage and layout. Batch voiceovers. Generate only the scene you cannot record.
