What this playbook assumes: you've run the diagnostic and landed on Track A — you want an asset that sells while you sleep, not client calls. No audience, no camera, no existing following required.

The trap on Track A isn't building the product — it's spending three weeks "getting it right" before anyone's seen it. This playbook is built to force a live storefront by day 5, because a live product with zero sales teaches you more than a perfect product still in drafts. First sale typically lands 19–24 days after launch — this playbook only covers getting to live; the roadmap module covers what happens after.

01
45 min

Validate the idea before you build anything

Don't build what nobody wants. A good product idea answers one of two questions for a specific person: "I want to achieve X" or "I want to avoid Y." "I want a PDF about productivity" answers neither — it's a topic, not a transformation. Run your idea through the prompt below before you write a single page.

Product validation prompt
I have a product idea: [describe your idea in one sentence].

Test it against these questions and be honest, not encouraging:
1. Does this answer "I want to achieve X" or "I want to avoid Y" for a specific person — not a general topic?
2. Who exactly is this for? Describe them in one sentence — their situation, not just a demographic.
3. What do they currently do instead of buying something like this? Why does that fall short?
4. Give this idea a specific, sellable name and a one-line promise.
5. Score this idea 1-10 on "would a stranger stop scrolling for this" and explain the score.

If the score is under 7, suggest 2 sharper alternatives using the same topic.
Paste into ChatGPT or Claude. Don't skip the "score under 7" step — a sharper idea now saves a wasted build tomorrow.
Rule for today: pick a narrow, specific idea over a broad, impressive-sounding one. "A 7-day content calendar for hair salons" outsells "the ultimate social media guide" every time — narrow means the buyer instantly knows it's for them.
02
90 min

Build the thing — 5 to 10 pages, genuinely useful

A tight, actionable 10-pager beats a bloated 80-pager every time — length signals effort, not value, and buyers judge digital products on whether they got a fast result, not a big file. Pick the format that fits your idea best:

PDF guide

A focused walkthrough with clear steps. Fastest to produce, works for almost any "how to" idea.

Template or swipe file pack

Fill-in-the-blank assets — captions, email scripts, contracts. Buyers want to copy, not learn.

Mini-course (5-part email or video)

A short sequence delivered over days. Higher perceived value, slightly more setup.

Checklist or framework bundle

A structured system — steps, checkboxes, a decision framework. Feels actionable in under a minute of skimming.

Product builder prompt
Build a [format: PDF guide / template pack / mini-course / checklist bundle] called "[working title]" that helps [specific person] achieve [specific result].

Structure it as:
1. A short intro (3-4 sentences) naming the problem and promising the specific outcome
2. [5-8] sections, each with a clear header and 2-4 actionable paragraphs or a fill-in template
3. One "quick win" the reader can act on in under 10 minutes, positioned early
4. A closing checklist summarizing every action step in the product

Keep total length to 5-10 pages. Write in plain, direct language — no filler, no generic advice a reader could get from a free blog post. Every section should feel like it was built specifically for [specific person], not for a general audience.
Run this once for a full draft, then a second pass asking Claude or ChatGPT to "cut anything that doesn't change what the reader does next."
03
60 min

Set up Gumroad and write the product page

Your product page needs exactly five things: a hook headline, three benefit bullets, a "what's inside" list, a price, and a guarantee. Nothing else moves the needle at this stage — resist adding testimonials or an FAQ section before you have either.

Landing page prompt
Write Gumroad product page copy for "[product name]" — [one-line description of what it is and who it's for].

Give me:
1. A hook headline (under 10 words) that leads with the outcome, not the format
2. 3 benefit bullets, each starting with a verb, each tied to a specific result
3. A "what's inside" list (bullet per section/asset in the product)
4. One sentence positioning the price as a fair trade for the result, not the page count
5. A simple guarantee line (e.g. "not useful in 10 minutes? full refund, no questions")

Tone: direct, confident, zero hype words like "ultimate" or "game-changing."
Paste the output straight into your Gumroad product description field.
Pricing: price low for your first sales — $[30]–$[50] — to earn reviews and social proof fast. Raise it once you have 5+ sales or a testimonial. Your first $500 usually comes from 5 × $50 products, 2 × $100 products, or one $150 product plus 7 × $50 — total units matter more than one big win.

04
120 min

Create 3 promo pieces around the #1 transformation

Pick the single biggest transformation your product delivers — not a feature list, one outcome — and build all three pieces around it. Playbook C covers the full faceless content system; here's a fast version to get your first post live today.

30-second promo script
[0-3s] Hook: "Nobody told [audience] this about [problem]."
[3-10s] Problem: name the frustration in one line your audience feels weekly.
[10-20s] Solution: "That's exactly why I built [product name] — [specific result] in [timeframe]."
[20-30s] CTA: "Link in bio — it's $[price]."
Record as screen + voiceover using Google Omni Flash, or text-on-screen over b-roll in CapCut. Export 1080×1920 with captions on.
Do this 3 times with 3 different hooks — a problem-hook, a curiosity-hook, and a result-hook — using the same product and CTA. You won't know which angle lands until you post; that's the point of shipping 3, not 1.
05
60 min

Schedule and launch

Post piece one today. Schedule pieces two and three for the next two days rather than posting all three at once — a live storefront needs sustained signal, not a single burst.

PostWhenAction after posting
Piece 1 — problem-hookTodayReply to every comment within the first hour; that window is when the algorithm decides reach.
Piece 2 — curiosity-hookTomorrowCross-post to Reels and Shorts using the same file.
Piece 3 — result-hookDay afterCheck analytics on piece 1 — note which hook got the most watch-through.
You're live. From here, the First $500 Roadmap module takes over — daily posting, iterating on your winning hook, and scaling toward your first sale, which typically lands day 19–24.
