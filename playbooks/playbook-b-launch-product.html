Free workflow

ChatGPT or Claude Free for structure. Google Docs or Canva Free for the file. One eligible storefront with no mandatory monthly subscription.

Use the storefront test mode where available. Confirm country support, fees, refund settings, delivery, and payouts before launch.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally handles longer source material. Canva Pro optionally adds brand and design features. Neither replaces buyer research.

Spend first on a demonstrable production bottleneck. A paid subscription does not validate an idea or supply customers.

Subscriptions do not include a promise of income. Check the live plan and make the same quality checks.

01

Find evidence of a narrow problem

Choose one buyer and one task. Collect five actual questions, review excerpts, or interview notes. Record sources and dates. Check how people solve the problem now. Ask three relevant people to react to a small sample before building.

Copy and adapt
Here is actual research: [sources and observations]. Separate evidence, inference, and unknowns for [product idea]. Identify a repeated problem, current alternatives, and a narrow product promise. Suggest three interview questions. Do not infer willingness to pay from an AI score.
Produce thisAn evidence worksheet and a one-sentence promise.
Pass checkThe promise is tied to a real task. At least one potential buyer reviewed a sample.
02

Define the smallest complete product

Choose a template pack, focused guide, workbook, or short tutorial. List the exact assets and success criteria. Include instructions, a worked example, and a checklist. Avoid a broad course generated from an outline alone.

Copy and adapt
Design a minimum complete [format] for [buyer] to do [task]. Use [research]. Output an asset list, section outline, a ten-minute first task, one filled example, and quality criteria. Flag anything requiring specialist expertise or verification.
Produce thisA product specification you can inspect before writing.
Pass checkThe buyer knows how to start and what a finished result looks like.
03

Produce and review every asset

Draft one section at a time. Edit generic advice. Test each template yourself using real or clearly fictional inputs. Export, open on mobile, and check links. Download the filled product example below as a reference.

Copy and adapt
Write section [section] using [verified inputs]. Include exact actions, one filled example, and a pass/fail check. Avoid invented statistics. Mark missing information. Then critique the draft for vague advice, unsupported claims, and steps a beginner cannot execute.
Produce thisA complete PDF or template pack with instructions.
Pass checkYou followed the instructions and reached the stated output. No placeholder remains in the buyer file.
04

Set price and delivery

Compare a few relevant offers using live sources. Choose an introductory price as a test, not a market fact. Document fees and net receipts. Configure one-time or recurring billing correctly and show refund terms you will honor.

Copy and adapt
Use these competitor observations: [URLs, prices, inclusions, dates]. Compare my product [assets]. Suggest three test prices with reasoning. Calculate gross and net under [verified fees]. Distinguish one-time payment from subscription. Do not invent competitor claims.
Produce thisA pricing rationale and configured checkout.
Pass checkBilling matches the product promise and the buyer receives the right file.
05

Write and test the product page

Show the outcome, buyer, contents, sample, limitations, price, and refund policy. Include authentic proof if you have it. Test checkout and receipt delivery in the platform test environment. A self-purchase and refund do not prove a payout schedule.

Copy and adapt
Write a concise product page for [product] using [verified contents]. Include buyer, problem, outcome, exact inclusions, screenshot suggestion, price, access instructions, actual refund policy, and one CTA. No invented students, income results, scarcity, or endorsements.
Produce thisA working page, sample preview, and delivery test.
Pass checkA new visitor understands what they buy and how to use it.

Its outcome is planning a week of salon posts, not guaranteed bookings. A complete filled version is linked below. Suggested practice launch price: $19, an illustration to test against actual demand. When you get stuck

AI likes the idea, buyers do not

Interview people and inspect alternatives. Replace the assumption with evidence or narrow the task.

Traffic but no purchases

Check buyer fit, page clarity, sample quality, checkout, and objections. Improve one item before generating another product.

Buyer cannot use the file

Add a quick-start page and filled example. Test with someone unfamiliar with the product.
