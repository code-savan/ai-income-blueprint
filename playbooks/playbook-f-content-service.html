Free workflow

ChatGPT or Claude Free, Google Docs/Sheets, Canva Free, and client-owned photos. Start with seven posts and three designs.

Pexels is a fallback for illustrative imagery. Confirm free elements and download your work before promising a format.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally stores longer brand context. Canva Pro optionally adds brand controls and approved premium elements.

Higgsfield optionally creates scene inserts from approved product images. Agree asset rights and production costs separately.

Subscriptions do not include a promise of income. Check the live plan and make the same quality checks.

01

Collect a usable client brief

Ask for services, prices, opening hours, target audience, booking link, brand voice, approver, approved photos, and prohibited claims. Clarify who posts and who answers messages. Download the intake template and complete the fictional example first.

Copy and adapt
Turn this intake [facts] into a one-page content brief. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and questions. Include brand voice, audience, services, CTA, available photos, approval contact, and exclusions. Stop and ask questions if the booking link or offer details are missing.
Produce thisAn approved brief with every essential detail.
Pass checkThe client confirms the information you will publish.
02

Plan seven posts with different jobs

Give each post a purpose: explain a service, answer a question, show a process, or invite a booking inquiry. Use a mix of useful education and relevant promotion. Match topics to available photos. Do not claim an arbitrary posting frequency creates bookings.

Copy and adapt
From [approved brief], create a seven-day calendar. Columns: day, topic, reader question, format, visual asset, caption angle, CTA, approval status. Use confirmed services only. Vary the job of each post. Do not invent promotions or before/after results.
Produce thisSeven calendar rows ready for client review.
Pass checkEvery post has a distinct purpose and a feasible visual.
03

Draft and improve the captions

Draft from the approved facts. Replace generic praise with specific useful guidance. Read aloud. Verify prices, spelling, dates, contact information, and business claims. Keep the client voice consistent. Show one raw-to-edited example in the pack.

Copy and adapt
Write captions for [approved calendar] using [brief]. Each has a clear first line, two useful detail sentences, and one CTA. Then audit every factual claim against the brief. Mark unsupported details rather than filling them in. Provide an edited version and explain changes.
Produce thisSeven captions plus a claim check.
Pass checkA reader knows what to do next and every business fact matches the brief.
04

Build three reusable designs

Create one service card, one FAQ graphic, and one process post in Canva. Use client photos where possible. Set the size, margins, contrast, typography, and logo placement. Export PNG and provide editable access only under agreed sharing terms.

Copy and adapt
Give three Canva design briefs from [calendar]. For each specify headline, supporting text, photo, layout, emphasis, and export format. Use a clear hierarchy and short text. Do not add unapproved discounts or performance claims.
Produce thisThree finished designs and source files.
Pass checkThe client sees the correct business identity and all text is readable on mobile.
05

Package and hand off

Create folders for brief, calendar, captions, designs, and instructions. Include posting order, filenames, and one revision form. Record a short walkthrough showing how to use the pack. Deliver on the agreed date and log approval.

Copy and adapt
Write a handoff message for [client] with [deliverables]. Explain where files live, suggested posting order, who posts, how to request the agreed revision, and the approval deadline. Add a three-minute walkthrough outline. Keep it clear and practical.
Produce thisA complete delivery folder and approval request.
Pass checkThe owner can use the pack without another explanation call.
06

Report and propose the next scope

Collect actual reach, clicks, and inquiries if you have access. Say which metrics are unavailable. Ask what the owner found useful. Propose a recurring package only if there is an ongoing need. Ask permission to feature the work and quote feedback.

Copy and adapt
Write a weekly report from [actual metrics]. Separate observed outcomes from assumptions. Summarize delivered assets, client feedback, and one next experiment. If tracking is missing, state that. Do not attribute every booking to social content.
Produce thisA factual report and an optional next-month scope.
Pass checkNo invented uplift or causal claim appears in the report.

When you get stuck

Client gives no photos

Ask for an approved phone photo set or agree an illustrative stock route. Do not invent product or treatment results.

Too many revisions

Use one approver, explicit acceptance criteria, and the agreed revision round. Quote new scope separately.

AI tone sounds generic

Supply sample client captions and edit the first line into specific customer language.
