Before you begin

Start with the tools you have.

The output stays the same. Choose how you’ll produce it.

Free workflow

Use a product you own or a program-provided demo you have permission to use. Apply only to programs supporting your country and payout method.

Film an honest comparison or demonstration and edit with available free tools. Commission is not instant cash.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally compares supplied program terms. Higgsfield optionally supplies clearly illustrative context, never fabricated product use.

Pay for samples or production only after checking eligibility, commission, attribution, refund clawbacks, and payout timing. Avoid upfront purchases whose cost exceeds a realistic test budget.

Check the current plan and your actual bottleneck before spending.

01

Check eligibility before choosing a route

Open the platform’s current country-specific rules and account eligibility page. Confirm age, location, identity, followers where required, and payment method. US TikTok Shop rules apply to US-based creators and must not be assumed to cover Nigeria.

Produce thisAn eligibility checklist and go/no-go decision.
Pass checkYou use your real location and identity. No borrowed account or location workaround.
Your working prompt
Summarize these official program terms [text and source URL/date]. List eligibility, country, account type, payout method, commission timing, and missing requirements. Do not assume access from follower count alone. If unavailable, suggest an eligible independent affiliate route or a service offer.
02

Inspect the offer and unit economics

Read the commission terms, product quality, returns, price, cookie or attribution rules, and brand restrictions. Check real reviews and your own experience. Compare the cost of a sample with potential commission and the chance of refunds.

Produce thisOne offer with documented terms and a budget.
Pass checkYou understand how a sale becomes an approved commission and when it becomes withdrawable.
Your working prompt
Compare these verified offers [program terms and product observations]. Show commission per valid sale, costs, restrictions, payout delay, and risks. Mark unknowns. Recommend a small test only when eligibility and product quality are clear.
03

Create an honest demonstration

Show the actual product and an observable use. State limits and who it suits. Disclose the affiliate relationship clearly. Do not produce a synthetic customer pretending to have used it. AI is suitable for planning and illustrative scenes.

Produce thisA truthful demo script with disclosure.
Pass checkThe footage and experience support the claims.
Your working prompt
Write a 30-second demo for [product I have used] from [observations]. Include use, one benefit I verified, one limitation, who it suits, and a clear affiliate disclosure. No invented personal experience, medical claims, or performance results.
04

Publish through the eligible route

For TikTok Shop, use the account’s Creator Center and approved Product Marketplace/showcase controls, then tag the eligible product. Check the market’s current posting and product rules. For an independent program, use its supplied trackable link and permitted channels.

Produce thisA published demo with a valid attributed product/link.
Pass checkThe link opens the intended product and the relationship is disclosed.
Your working prompt
Make a publishing checklist from [official instructions] for my eligible route [route]. Include product/link selection, disclosure, content review, tracking, and a final preview. Do not invent interface labels or bypass account restrictions.
05

Review approved commissions, not views

Record clicks, attributed orders, returns, approved commission, costs, and funds available. Test another angle only after inspecting the product and tracking. Use the same content skill to offer product creative to businesses if affiliate access is unavailable.

Produce thisAn affiliate review and a stop/continue decision.
Pass checkA high view count is not reported as income and pending commission is not counted as cash.
Your working prompt
Analyze this actual affiliate data [rows]. Separate orders, pending commission, approved commission, refunds, and withdrawable funds. Calculate contribution after sample and production costs. Suggest one controlled follow-up experiment.
Finished reference / fictional practice inputs

Worked example: access unavailable, skill still useful

A creator in Nigeria should check the account’s current local eligibility instead of relying on US follower rules. If Shop access is unavailable, use a program that expressly accepts your location or offer businesses a scoped product-video service using H. For practice economics, a $30 product with 10% commission produces $3 per valid order. Twenty approved orders produce $60 gross commission before sample cost, production, refunds, and fees. This is arithmetic, not expected earnings.

When you get stuck
No TikTok Shop access

Use an eligible independent program or the product-creative service route. Do not spend a week on an unavailable channel.

Product cannot be tested

Do not claim personal use. Choose a product you can demonstrate or produce clearly labeled informational content.

Commissions remain pending

Read the program’s fulfillment, return, and payout conditions. Do not infer a next-day payout.
