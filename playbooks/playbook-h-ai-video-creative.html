Free workflow

Use client-owned product footage or film your own hands-on demo. Add licensed stock only for context. Write with ChatGPT or Claude Free and edit in available free VN/CapCut features.

If you cannot access the product, offer a storyboard or illustrative concept rather than claiming a genuine product-use demonstration.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally expands the script and storyboard. Higgsfield optionally generates short scene inserts from an approved product image.

Higgsfield prices and model access vary by plan. Read the current monthly card, available credits, and Generate-button cost. Use the capacity calculator under Tools before quoting. Web unlimited access is model-specific and does not apply to automated generations.

Subscriptions do not include a promise of income. Check the live plan and make the same quality checks.

01

Agree the creative brief and rights

Define audience, product, goal, platform, duration, approved claims, reference style, music rights, image rights, usage, and revision count. Ask whether synthetic scenes are acceptable. Offer creative production, not guaranteed leads.

Copy and adapt
Turn [client facts] into a 30-second creative brief. Include audience, problem, approved product benefits, CTA, usage, deliverables, revisions, and asset rights. List missing facts and claims requiring evidence. Do not invent customer experiences.

Pass checkYou have permission to use the product images, identity, voice, and audio.
02

Storyboard before generating

Write a six-shot story: hook, problem, product, demonstration, detail, CTA. Separate real evidence from illustrative scenes. Give duration, text, and motion for each shot. Design three hooks around one consistent body.

Copy and adapt
Storyboard a 30-second video from [brief]. Six shots with time, visual source, voiceover, text overlay, and action. Label REAL DEMO or ILLUSTRATION. Provide three truthful hooks. Keep product labels and claims unchanged.
Produce thisSix-shot storyboard and three hook variants.
Pass checkEvery shot has a purpose and the product claim is supported.
03

Build the free route first

Record close-ups with steady light and a plain background. Use licensed stock for context, never as false product evidence. Place shots in the editor, record voice, add captions, and export a rough cut before considering AI.

Copy and adapt
Adapt [storyboard] to a no-subscription production plan. Use own screen/product footage, still-image movement, and licensed stock. Give exact shot substitutions, recording instructions, and edit order. Preserve the original claims.
Produce thisOne rough edit made without an AI video subscription.
Pass checkThe product is recognizable and the demonstration is honest.
04

Generate only the missing scenes

For the paid route, upload approved assets into a currently available Higgsfield image/video workflow. Generate a short test at the intended ratio. Check labels, anatomy, motion, and consistency before more attempts. Stop when the scene budget is used.

Copy and adapt
Create a short image-to-video prompt from [approved product image description]. Scene: [scene]. Camera: [one movement]. Duration: [duration]. Preserve label, product shape, and brand colors. Natural lighting. Do not add new claims, extra products, or fake testimonials. Give a simpler fallback.
Produce thisOne accepted scene with actual credits and attempts logged.
Pass checkYou inspect the output frame by frame and reject altered labels or invented demonstrations.
05

Edit, export, and package variants

Combine approved scenes with the real demonstration. Add voice, captions, licensed music, and the CTA. Export 1080×1920 for the agreed platform. Package three opening variants, one main body, source notes, and a handoff guide.

Copy and adapt
Create a delivery checklist for [agreed creative]. Include duration, ratio, captions, spelling, claim accuracy, product consistency, music license, variants, filenames, and client approval. Add a handoff note explaining which shots are generated.
Produce thisThree finished hook variants and a documented delivery.
Pass checkThe client understands what was generated and what was recorded.
Filled demonstration · fictional inputs

Worked example: a fictional desk organizer

30-second concept: 0–3s show a cluttered desk, 3–7s name the difficulty finding small items, 7–13s show the real organizer and its compartments, 13–20s record placing pens and notes inside, 20–26s use an illustrative slow product push-in, 26–30s show the product page CTA. The paid scene prompt keeps the product shape and label intact. Budget illustration: 1,000 credits, 10 credits per attempt, three attempts per accepted shot, two generated shots per edit gives floor(1000 / 60) = 16 edits before image or upscale costs. These inputs are hypothetical, not a live subscription allowance. Use the calculator with your actual plan.

When you get stuck

Generations distort the product

Use the real photo or filmed footage. A still with subtle movement is a valid final scene.

Credits disappear quickly

Reduce generated shots, retries, or resolution. Log actual cost. Account for image generation and upscaling too.

No leads from the creative

Check offer, audience, landing page, and lead follow-up. Creative delivery alone does not establish campaign results.
