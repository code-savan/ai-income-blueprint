START / TOOL ROUTE
Before you begin

Start with the tools you have.

The output stays the same. Choose how you’ll produce it.

Free workflow

Start with a free writing tool and one free editor: Docs, Sheets, Slides, Canva or a video editor. Use your own files or assets you have permission to use.

Finish one practice output before buying anything. Free-tier limits can change. Save your work and test a free export first.

Paid workflow

Paid AI, design or research tools are optional. Pay for a specific problem, such as a longer source file or repeated editing task.

Read the current plan, country support and credit cost in Tools & costs. Budget for retries. A subscription is not a fixed number of finished deliverables or sales.

Check the current plan and your actual bottleneck before spending.

Compare tools & credit costs ↗

Pick one reader and one question

Use a question a likely client or buyer actually asked. For a service, teach how to spot or fix one problem. For a product, show one task inside it. For an affiliate item, show fit, use and a limitation. Keep the lesson small.

Your working prompt
Audience: [buyer]. Actual question: [question and source]. Offer: [chosen service/product]. Suggest five carousel angles that answer this question with a visible example. Give one sentence describing what the reader can do after seven slides. No unsupported income or platform-reach claims.
02

Write the seven slides

Use: 1 question, 2 common mistake, 3 first action, 4 second action, 5 filled example, 6 check, 7 next step. Keep headings short. Put the exact click or prompt where needed. Do not turn every slide into a product claim.
