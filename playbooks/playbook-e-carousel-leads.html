START / TOOL ROUTE
Before you begin

Start with the tools you have.

The output stays the same. Choose how you’ll produce it.

Free workflow

Draft with ChatGPT or Claude Free. Design in Canva Free at 1080×1350. Use owned imagery or relevant Pexels photos, and use only free elements.

Duplicate pages, vary the layout, and export PNG. Review each slide at phone size before posting.

Paid workflow

Claude Pro optionally helps adapt a longer source document. Canva Pro optionally supplies brand controls and licensed assets.

Higgsfield is optional for a visual demonstration illustration. Use real output screenshots as evidence and disclose generated scenes where relevant.

Check the current plan and your actual bottleneck before spending.

01

Choose one reader and one decision

Pick a question your buyer faces: how to find a prospect, how to write a caption, or what goes in a client pack. Make the carousel solve one part of the problem. Choose one next step suited to the content.

Produce thisOne carousel brief with reader, promise, and CTA.
Pass checkThe reader gains something useful even without buying.
Your working prompt
Reader: [specific situation]. Problem: [problem]. Offer: [offer]. Give five carousel angles teaching one useful action. For each identify the demonstration, desired reader action, and honest hook. No claims about algorithm preference or guaranteed reach.
02

Write seven slides with a payoff

Open with a clear tension. Show the mistake, method, worked example, and next action. Keep one idea per slide. Use short headings and only the supporting text necessary to understand the action.

Produce thisA seven-slide script and visual plan.
Pass checkThe sequence is useful in order, with no duplicate point or unsupported result.
Your working prompt
Write seven slides from [source]. Structure: hook, mistake, step one, step two, filled example, checklist, CTA. Each heading under ten words, body under 35 words. Suggest a visual for each. Ground every claim in the supplied evidence.
03

Set a small design system

In Canva create a custom 1080×1350 design. Pick two font families, two brand colors plus a neutral, and a consistent label. Use large readable headlines and safe margins. Build text, photo, screenshot, and comparison layouts.

Produce thisFour reusable page layouts and a consistent style.
Pass checkText contrasts with the background and each layout serves its content.
Your working prompt
For this seven-slide script [script], propose a layout per slide using text-only, screenshot, photo-overlay, or before/after. State hierarchy, emphasis, and whitespace. Avoid repeating the same heading position. Prioritize phone readability.
04

Add actual evidence and export

Place the prompt screenshot, tracker row, or final output where it teaches the method. Crop account details. Use a dark overlay on busy photos. Download all pages as PNG, inspect order, and open them on a phone.

Produce thisSeven correctly ordered images and one caption.
Pass checkEvery image is legible, licensed, and consistent. No person in stock imagery is presented as a customer.
Your working prompt
Review this slide copy and visual plan [details]. Flag vague teaching, small text risk, missing context, duplicated layouts, and unverifiable claims. Give exact replacements. Keep source screenshots as the evidence.
05

Connect the carousel to a lead path

Use I to deliver a useful sample manually or through an eligible automation tool. If asking people to comment a keyword, have a working response process. Record profile visits, saves, clicks, conversations, and purchases where available.

Produce thisA published carousel and a verified sample-delivery process.
Pass checkThe next step works and you measure buyer actions without assuming saves equal sales.
Your working prompt
Write a caption for [carousel] and a manual response to someone requesting [sample]. Be clear about what they receive. Use one CTA. Add a follow-up question only after delivering the requested resource. Do not imply automation exists if it does not.

When you get stuck
Lots of words on each slide

Cut the slide to one decision and move supporting detail to the caption or linked guide.

Pretty but no teaching

Insert a filled example and show why you changed the output.

No leads

Check buyer relevance and sample delivery. Compare actions per reach across several posts rather than one.
