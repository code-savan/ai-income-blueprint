What would you actually deliver for a salon?

A useful content pack starts with confirmed facts, then turns them into a calendar, captions, designs, and a clear handoff. Here is a small excerpt you can inspect before deciding whether the Blueprint fits.

1. Start with the brief.

Practice business: Luna Braids, Abuja. Services: consultation, protective-style appointment, braid-care advice. Hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 10:00–18:00. Price rule: quote after confirming service details. CTA: owner-approved booking link. Photos: owner-approved assets with permission.

These are fictional inputs. Replace them with confirmed client facts. Leave unknown details blank and ask the owner.

2. Give the post a clear job.

PostReader questionOutput
ConsultationHow do I choose a style?Service card + relevant inquiry CTA
PreparationWhat should I confirm beforehand?FAQ graphic + owner-approved instructions
ProcessWhat information does the salon need?Reference, date, questions + clear next step

3. Edit the AI draft.

Raw draft

Generic praise and invented urgency

“Get world-class braids at the best salon in Abuja. Book now before slots disappear!”

The brief does not support the ranking or limited availability.

Edited version

A useful, supported next step

“Not sure which braid style to ask for? Bring a reference photo and tell us about your routine. A consultation helps us discuss the style and maintenance questions before you book. Ask through [approved booking link].”

4. Check it before handoff.

Does every fact match the brief? Are the photos approved? Does the booking link work? Can the owner tell which caption and design to post? Have you explained the revision scope?

This preview demonstrates a deliverable. It is not a client case study, earnings claim, or promise that a post creates bookings.
