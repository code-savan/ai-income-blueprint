02 / Fix the draft

Make the claim match the brief.

Fitness coach / raw → edited
Raw draft

Transform your body fast with the best coach. Only two spaces left!

Edited from confirmed facts

New to coached sessions? Tell us what you want to work on and ask how an introductory session runs. We explain the format and confirm whether it suits you before agreeing a booking.

Repair business / raw → edited
Raw draft

We fix every device instantly at the lowest price. Book before prices go up!

Edited from confirmed facts

Not sure whether your device needs a repair? Describe the issue and the device model when requesting an assessment. We explain the assessment process and confirm a quote before any repair work is agreed.

Online shop / raw → edited
Raw draft

The organizer that doubles productivity overnight. Everyone loves it!

Edited from confirmed facts

Need a place for pens and small notes? This three-compartment organizer measures 20 × 10 × 8 cm in our practice fact sheet. Check the dimensions against your desk space and review the product page before ordering.

03 / Try it yourself

Write one better caption.

  1. Choose a business or product you understand.
  2. Write its reader, actual offer, approved facts and next step.
  3. Answer one reader question in a short caption.
  4. Remove claims the facts do not support. Check the final link.

These are fictional demonstrations, not commissioned work or earnings stories.
