Choose the work you will sell

Help me define a small service using [chosen service], [skills I can demonstrate], [hours available] and [buyer types I understand]. Ask up to five questions if needed. Recommend one buyer type and a bounded package. Give exact files, required client inputs, what is excluded, practice time and one quality check. Write a plain offer sentence. No promised income, bookings or client results.

Check: You can explain what the buyer gets and make a practice version yourself.
