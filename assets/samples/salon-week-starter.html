Salon Week Starter: a complete reusable product

A fictional product prototype to inspect and adapt. It helps plan a week of salon content. It does not promise bookings. Suggested $19 price in the playbook is a practice assumption, not proven demand. The complete instructions, filled example, worksheet, and replies are below.

Start here: how to use this product

  1. Gather your services, opening hours, booking URL, approved photos, and claim restrictions.
  2. Fill the brief worksheet below. Do not publish until essential facts are confirmed.
  3. Read the filled calendar and captions. Choose the post purpose, then substitute your facts.
  4. Complete your blank calendar. Make the three layouts in your editor using the design references.
  5. Check the QA list, preview on mobile, and confirm the booking link.
  6. Save or print your completed work before closing this page. The editable fields do not auto-save.

Booking inquiry replies

New inquiry: Thanks for reaching out. Which service are you considering, and what day would suit you? Please send a reference if useful. We will confirm details and availability before agreeing the appointment.

Preparation question: Thanks. Please tell us your chosen service so the owner can confirm the right preparation instructions. We do not want to give you generic instructions that may not apply.

Price question: We quote after confirming the service details. Send [required details] through [approved booking channel], and the owner will explain the price and next step.

Quality checklist

  • Services and hours match the approved brief.
  • No invented prices, booking availability, promotions, or hair-growth claims.
  • Replace every [booking link] and open the actual destination.
  • Obtain permission for client photos and identifiable people.
  • Read every design on a phone. Verify text and export dimensions.
  • Confirm the calendar, filenames, posting owner, and revision limit.
  • Record reach and inquiries only if actual measurements are available.

What to measure

Record published posts, actual link clicks, qualified inquiries, and confirmed appointments separately. Note where an inquiry came from if the person tells you. Do not attribute every booking to the calendar.
