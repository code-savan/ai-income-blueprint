3. Use the two-stage research prompt if helpful. Request a small batch first. Keep inaccessible sources as unknown.
4. Open every source and check names, location, business relevance and contact route. Remove duplicates and personal details.
5. Deliver ten checked rows plus search notes and limits. Do not send messages or claim purchase intent as part of a research-only package.
