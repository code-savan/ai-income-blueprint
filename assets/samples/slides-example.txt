MAKE YOUR VERSION
1. Ask for the current deck and the one thing the audience should understand or do.
2. Open Google Slides → Blank presentation. Set 16:9 unless the client specifies another format.
3. Turn each slide title into one clear sentence. Keep three short supporting points or one visual.
4. Apply two fonts, matching heading positions and one accent color. Preserve the meaning of every number.
5. Export PDF, check slides at presentation size and deliver the editable deck with an agreed revision round.

FINAL CHECK
No changed figures, text is readable at normal viewing size, and slide order supports the speaking goal.
