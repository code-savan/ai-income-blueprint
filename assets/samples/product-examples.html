Freelancer project-start kit

A printable workbook plus editable project and delivery checklist.

First useful task: Fill one vague client request into a clear project checklist.

Notion has an established freelance template category, including client and project tools. This confirms the format and competition, not demand for your specific kit.

Filled practice worksheet ↓Blank worksheet ↓

Build and test your version

  1. Open the included Freelancer Project Starter to see a complete example. Pick one buyer, such as a new freelance designer.

Simple monthly expense sheet

A manual income and expense Sheet with a category summary.

First useful task: Add three fictional expenses and show the correct total.

Notion offers budgets and expense trackers. The curated scope is a simple recording tool, not financial guidance.

Filled practice worksheet ↓Blank worksheet ↓

Build and test your version

  1. Ask potential users which categories they need. Keep the first version manual and in one currency.
  2. In Sheets add Date, Description, Type, Category and Amount. Use dropdowns for Income/Expense and simple categories.
  3. Name the first tab Transactions. On a Summary tab, put Income, Expenses and Remaining in A2:A4. In B2 use =SUMIF(Transactions!C2:C,"Income",Transactions!E2:E). In B3 use =SUMIF(Transactions!C2:C,"Expense",Transactions!E2:E). In B4 use =B2-B3. If your locale needs semicolons, replace formula commas. Test five fictional entries and hand-calculate totals.
  4. Make an Example copy and a blank buyer copy. Protect formula cells where possible and explain how to add a row.
  5. Test a zero-spend month, a negative adjustment and a new category. Export instructions. Do not ask for bank login details.

Check your finished workHand totals equal formula totals, currency is clear and adding a row does not silently break the summary.
