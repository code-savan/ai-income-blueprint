Service packages are editorial adaptations of established creative/marketing/admin work. Product sources establish formats, competition or broader category signals. They do not validate a new offer or prove willingness to pay. The buyer test is part of the route.

## Important cross-checks

- Manus Wide Research is paid, not a promised free parallel 20-hour task. Use smaller free/manual research batches and verify every source. https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11960169-what-is-wide-research
- Z.ai chat and its billed developer APIs are distinct. Actual search tools depend on the chat account. https://chat.z.ai/ and https://docs.z.ai/guides/tools/web-search
- Vercel Hobby excludes commercial use. Paid client sites need a suitable hosting plan. The novice static-preview guide uses Cloudflare Pages Direct Upload, with its limits explained. https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby and https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/get-started/direct-upload/
- TikTok Creative Center’s older Top Products URL redirects to its newer creative hub. No current top-product ranking was extracted or claimed. Shopify category research supports three broad categories. Specific affiliate item choices remain inspection candidates, not verified sales winners.
- US TikTok Shop eligibility is not global. The product route checks country/account/program/payment eligibility before sample spending. https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6939143037667118
- Google Voice personal/standalone local-number access is US-only. Workspace add-ons support a listed set of countries. No Nigeria local-number eligibility is assumed. https://support.google.com/voice/answer/9249103?hl=en
- Calling rates vary by origin/destination and mobile/landline. Talk360 is prepaid, not unlimited free calling or a US-number guarantee. https://www.talk360.com/rates
- Whop sale settlement and bank payout are separate. The plan does not promise Thursday-to-Friday or Friday-to-Saturday cash. https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/payout-methods and https://docs.whop.com/manage-your-business/manage-payouts/troubleshoot-payouts

## Limits of this review

Live source documents and marketplace categories were reviewed. No fresh customer interviews, paid client orders, real affiliate-item inspections or bank payout transaction were performed. Example businesses and worksheet figures are labeled fictional. Practice build times are planning estimates. The user should record actual work and test with likely buyers rather than present those exercises as customer proof.
