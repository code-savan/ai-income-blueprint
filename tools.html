Plan your actual production budget

Estimate usable video edits.

The starting inputs are hypothetical, not a Higgsfield subscription. Enter your displayed model cost, expected retries and shots per finished edit. Real capacity may be lower.

How the estimate works

Credits per edit = attempt cost × attempts × generated shots + other credits. Edits = floor(available credits ÷ credits per edit). Subscription cost per estimated edit = plan cost ÷ edits. Labor, taxes and separate plans are excluded.
