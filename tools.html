How many usable video edits can your budget support?

The starting numbers are hypothetical, not a Higgsfield plan. Enter the actual displayed cost, expected retries, and generated shots per edit. Count image generation, upscales, and other credit costs. Real capacity may be lower.

Credits per edit = attempt cost × attempts × generated shots + other credits. Estimated edits = floor(credits available ÷ credits per edit). Subscription cost per estimated edit = plan cost ÷ edits. This excludes labor, taxes, separate plans, and failed work beyond your retry estimate.

A free workflow you can run today.

  1. Collect verified business facts and approved photos.
  2. Draft with ChatGPT or Claude Free. Save the output and edit it yourself.
  3. Make three designs with free Canva assets, or film your screen and own voice.
  4. Use Pexels only for supporting imagery with the license checked.
  5. Add captions manually if your editor’s automatic captions require a subscription.
  6. Deliver through a shareable folder and test access in another browser session.
